Home/Meal plans

Plans that survive a real week

Every plan assumes something will go wrong on Wednesday. Each one has a fallback meal built from ingredients already on the list, so a late meeting does not cost you $40 of wilted produce.

What a plan actually contains

Not a grid of stock photos. A plan is five dinners, one make-ahead breakfast and one weekend cook, chosen so that ingredients overlap deliberately — the herbs bought for Monday get used again on Thursday.

  • Aisle-sorted shopping list with quantities in both grams and cups, and a separate "check your cupboard first" section.
  • A Sunday prep block of 40 minutes, itemised. If you skip it, each weeknight gains about 12 minutes — the plan says so rather than pretending the prep is optional.
  • Timing chart per night showing which pan goes on first.
  • A named fallback for the night that goes wrong, using ingredients already bought.
  • Cost estimate per serving, benchmarked at a mid-market national supermarket in the previous month.
