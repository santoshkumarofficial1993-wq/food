Before you write

Six things we get asked constantly. If yours is here, you have your answer immediately.

What you did, what happened, and what equipment you used — especially oven, pan material and whether you weighed or measured by volume. That is almost always enough for us to find the gap in our instructions. Photos help but are not necessary. We do not treat these as complaints; they are the most useful mail we get.

Not in full. You are welcome to reference a recipe, describe it in your own words and link to it. Ingredient lists and method text are copyrighted as written. Licensing for commercial use is available — write to press@ and tell us what you have in mind. See the terms of use.

Often, yes — around a third of our test schedule comes from reader requests. Family recipe cards with missing details are especially welcome; reverse-engineering those is one of the more satisfying parts of the job. We cannot promise a timeline and we will tell you if we decide against it.

No. We do not publish guest posts, we do not sell links, and we do not insert links into existing articles for payment. We receive about forty of these requests a month and do not reply to them. Genuine advertising enquiries belong on our partners page.

Email privacy@skilletandstars.com from the address you signed up with and ask. We action deletion requests within 30 days and confirm when it is done. You do not need to give a reason, and asking does not affect anything else. Details in the privacy policy.
