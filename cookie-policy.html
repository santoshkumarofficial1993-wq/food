Cookie policy

Every cookie this site can set, what it is for, and how to turn it off. Nothing beyond the strictly necessary cookies is set before you choose.

Last updated: 12 January 2026. Read alongside our privacy policy.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. Related technologies — local storage, session storage and pixels — do similar jobs and are covered by this policy too. We use them for three things: keeping the site working, understanding which pages get read, and serving advertising.

Your choice, and how to change it

On your first visit you are asked to accept or reject non-essential cookies. Until you choose, only strictly necessary cookies are set and no advertising or analytics scripts are loaded at all.

You can change your mind at any time — use the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page. You can also clear or block cookies entirely in your browser settings; the site will continue to work, though your consent choice will be forgotten and you will be asked again.

Kitchen Pass members do not see advertising, so no advertising cookies are set on their sessions regardless of their consent choice.

Opting out of personalised advertising

  • Reject non-essential cookies in our consent notice, or change your choice via Cookie settings in the footer. Advertising may still appear, but it will be contextual rather than personalised.
  • Turn off ad personalisation across Google's products at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of many participating vendors at optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Enable a Global Privacy Control signal in your browser — we honour it automatically.
  • Become a Kitchen Pass member, which removes advertising from the site entirely.

Browser controls

Every major browser lets you view, delete and block cookies, usually under Settings → Privacy. Blocking all cookies will break the sign-in on membership accounts, and the consent notice will reappear on every visit because we have nowhere to record your answer.

Changes to this policy

If we add a cookie or change what an existing one does, this page is updated and the date at the top changes. Where the change affects consent, you will be asked again rather than opted in silently.

Questions

Write to privacy@skilletandstars.com. If you think a cookie is being set that is not listed here, please tell us — we would genuinely like to know.
