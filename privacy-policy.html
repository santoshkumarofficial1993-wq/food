1. Who we are

Skillet & Stars Media LLC ("Skillet & Stars", "we", "us") operates www.skilletandstars.com. Our registered address is 1120 Haywood Road, Suite 4, Asheville, NC 28806, United States. For any privacy matter, write to privacy@skilletandstars.com.

We are the data controller for the information described below. Where we use service providers who process data on our behalf, they act as processors under contract and may not use your information for their own purposes — with the exception of advertising partners, described in section 5, who act as independent controllers for the advertising data they collect.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Newsletter signup: your email address, and the date and source of the signup.
  • Membership account: name, email address, country, and billing information. Card details are entered directly with our payment processor and are never stored on our servers — we receive only the last four digits and the card type.
  • Contact and enquiry forms: name, email address, and the content of your message, including any recipe or order details you choose to include.
  • Shop orders: shipping address and order contents.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: IP address, browser user-agent, referring page, requested URL, and timestamp. Retained for 30 days for security and abuse prevention, then deleted.
  • Analytics: aggregated page views, approximate region (country and state level, not precise location), device type and session duration. We use privacy-focused analytics that do not build cross-site profiles of individuals.
  • Advertising data: collected by Google and its partners when advertising cookies are enabled with your consent — see section 5.
  • Local storage: your cookie consent choice, and any recipe preferences such as serving scale, stored in your own browser and never transmitted to us.

We do not collect health information, precise geolocation, biometric data, or any special category data. We do not ask for dietary or medical details, and we ask you not to send them.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. We group them into three categories, described in full in our cookie policy:

  • Strictly necessary — session security, load balancing, and remembering your cookie choice. These are set without consent because the site cannot function without them.
  • Analytics — aggregate measurement of which pages are read. Set only with consent.
  • Advertising — set by Google and its certified partners to serve and measure advertising. Set only with consent.

When you first visit, no analytics or advertising cookies are set until you make a choice in the consent notice. You can change that choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page, and you can clear cookies through your browser at any time.

5. Advertising, Google AdSense and third-party vendors

This site is supported in part by display advertising. Free readers see advertising; Kitchen Pass members do not. Advertising on this site is served by Google and by third-party vendors and ad networks that Google works with. The following applies to that advertising:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • If you do not consent, or you withdraw consent, advertising may still be shown but will be non-personalised — contextual, based on the page you are reading rather than a profile of you.

Advertising vendors act as independent controllers of the data they collect through these cookies. We do not receive your advertising profile, and we cannot access or delete data held by them — the opt-out links above are the effective route.

We block the following advertising categories on our inventory: alcohol, gambling, weight-loss and diet products, supplements making health claims, cryptocurrency, and political advertising.

6. Who we share information with

We share the minimum necessary with a short list of service providers:

  • Email delivery provider — to send the newsletter and transactional email.
  • Payment processor — to take payment and manage subscriptions. They are PCI-DSS compliant and hold the card data we never see.
  • Hosting and content delivery provider — to serve the site.
  • Fulfilment partner — name and shipping address only, for physical book orders.
  • Advertising partners — as described in section 5, subject to your consent.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to do so, or where necessary to establish or defend legal claims. If we are ever acquired, personal data would form part of the transferred assets and you would be notified before any change in how it is used.

7. How long we keep it

  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days.
  • Membership accounts: for the life of the account, plus seven years for the billing records we are required to keep for tax purposes.
  • Contact messages: 24 months, then deleted.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Shop orders: seven years, for tax and returns.

8. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Delete your information ("right to erasure").
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

Email privacy@skilletandstars.com from the address associated with your account. We action requests within 30 days and confirm when done. You do not need to give a reason, and exercising a right will never affect your membership or the service you receive.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland

You have the rights above under the GDPR and equivalent UK law, and you may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. Personalised advertising and analytics cookies are set only after you consent, in line with Google's EU user consent policy.

If you are in California

Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. Because personalised advertising may constitute "sharing" under California law, you may opt out by rejecting advertising cookies in our consent notice, using the Cookie settings link in the footer, or by emailing privacy@skilletandstars.com. We honour Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

Other US states

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have comparable rights of access, correction, deletion and opt-out, exercisable through the same address. Where a state provides an appeal mechanism for a refused request, you may appeal by replying to our decision email; we will respond within the statutory period.

9. Children

This site is intended for a general adult audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not serve personalised advertising to anyone we know to be a child. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, write to privacy@skilletandstars.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security and international transfers

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to personal data inside the company is limited to the people who need it, protected by multi-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure, but we will notify affected users and the relevant authority without undue delay if a breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights.

We are based in the United States, so information you send us is processed there. For transfers from the EEA, UK or Switzerland we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses with our providers, or their participation in an approved transfer framework.

11. How to contact us

Privacy questions and data requests: privacy@skilletandstars.com
Everything else: hello@skilletandstars.com
By post: Skillet & Stars Media LLC, 1120 Haywood Road, Suite 4, Asheville, NC 28806, United States.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled a request, tell us and a different person will review it. You retain the right to complain to your data protection authority regardless.

This policy is provided for transparency about our practices. It is not legal advice, and if you operate a similar site you should have your own policy reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.
