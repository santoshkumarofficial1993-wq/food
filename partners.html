What we sell

Three things, priced plainly. No rate card games, no "premium takeover" tiers that are the same inventory renamed.

Display advertising

Sold programmatically via Google AdSense and Google Ad Manager, plus a small amount of direct inventory.

  • Leaderboard, in-article and sidebar rectangle
  • Every unit labelled "Advertisement"
  • No interstitials, no auto-playing audio, no vignettes
  • Maximum three units per page

Direct display: $14 CPM, US-only targeting available.

Newsletter placement

One placement per issue, below the three recipes, marked as sponsored.

  • 184,000 subscribers, 48% open rate
  • Text plus one image, written by you
  • We will not write your copy in our voice
  • Two-week lead time

$1,900 per issue. Four-issue minimum unavailable — single issues only.

Sponsored recipe development

Maximum four per year. Labelled at the top of the page, in the feed, and in the newsletter.

  • Developed and tested to the same standard as editorial
  • You get approval on factual claims about your product only
  • You do not get approval on the recipe, the rating or the copy
  • If it fails our testing, we tell you and refund in full

From $12,000. Two of the last six were refunded.

The editorial firewall

This is the part most media kits leave out, so here it is in full.

  • Advertisers never see recipes before publication unless they have paid for a sponsored recipe, and then only their own.
  • No advertiser has ever had a recipe changed. If that ever happens, we will publish the fact.
  • Equipment tests are not for sale. No amount of spend gets a product into a test round, and appearing in a test round does not require any spend.
  • Sponsored content is labelled at the top, not at the bottom, and not only in small print. It is also excluded from our "best of" round-ups permanently.
  • We disclose affiliate relationships on every page carrying an affiliate link, above the products rather than in the footer.

Display advertising is served by Google and its certified partners under Google's publisher policies. We do not control which individual advertisers appear in programmatic slots, but we block categories: alcohol, gambling, weight-loss and diet products, cryptocurrency, and political advertising.

What we turn down

  • Weight-loss products, appetite suppressants and diet programmes
  • Supplements making health claims
  • Anything requiring us to imply a health outcome from a recipe
  • Undisclosed sponsorship, "gifted" arrangements or affiliate-only reviews
  • Link insertions into existing editorial — we get about 40 of these requests a month and decline all of them
  • AI-generated content placements

If you are emailing about a link insertion or a guest post, the answer is no, and a polite no takes us both less time than a follow-up sequence.
