How our equipment testing works

We buy every unit at retail with our own money. Nothing is sent to us for review, and nothing is kept without being paid for. Testing runs to a written protocol set before the units arrive, so we cannot quietly change the criteria to fit a result we like.

  • Minimum three months of daily use before anything is called a winner.
  • Measured, not felt. Surface thermometers, timed heat-up curves, weighed edge retention. The numbers are published even when they are dull.
  • Retested annually. Manufacturers change specifications quietly. Two of our previous winners have been withdrawn from this page for exactly that reason.
  • Losers named. We publish what failed and why, which is why our equipment posts are longer than our recipes.
